This page explains how to ask us to delete your Catholic Trivia account (published in Spanish as “Trivia Católica”) and all associated data. You don't need to have the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to hola@triviacatolica.com with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to sign in (Google or Apple). If you used Apple's “Hide My Email” or played as a guest, include your leaderboard display name so we can identify your account.
  3. We'll confirm receipt and delete your account and data within 30 days at most. We'll email you once it's done.
Important if you have Catholic Trivia Pro: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings (Subscriptions), so it doesn't renew.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identifier (Google, Apple or guest).
  • Your email and name, if we have them.
  • Your profile: display name, XP, streaks, lives and preferences.
  • Your progress on each level (scores and stars) and your leaderboard position.
  • The link between your account and your subscription in RevenueCat.
  • Your waitlist email, if you signed up.

Data that may be kept

  • Question reports: kept without any link to your account (anonymously), to maintain content quality.
  • Purchase records: Google, Apple and RevenueCat keep payment receipts for as long as their legal and tax obligations require.
  • Backups: data may remain in technical backups for up to 30 additional days, after which it is overwritten.

Deleting only some data

If you'd rather keep your account but delete something specific (for example, your display name or your progress), email hola@triviacatolica.com and tell us what you want removed.
