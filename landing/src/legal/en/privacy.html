This policy explains what personal data the Catholic Trivia app (iOS and Android, published in Spanish as “Trivia Católica”) and the website triviacatolica.com process, why we use it and what rights you have. If anything is unclear, write to us at hola@triviacatolica.com.

In short: you can play without signing up. We only store what the game needs to work (your progress, streak and leaderboard position). We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't use advertising trackers.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the Catholic Trivia team. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at hola@triviacatolica.com.

2. What data we collect

a) Guest account (no sign-up)

When you open the app for the first time, an anonymous account with a random identifier is created automatically. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number to start playing.

b) If you sign in with Google or Apple

If you choose to save your progress with Google or Apple, we receive an account identifier from that provider and, depending on what you authorize, your email address and name. With “Sign in with Apple” you can hide your real email. We never receive or store your password.

c) Game data

  • Display name on the leaderboard (if you don't choose one, you appear as “Peregrino”, Spanish for “Pilgrim”).
  • Experience points (XP), current and longest streak, date of your last game.
  • Completed levels, score and stars earned on each level.
  • Available lives and game preferences (for example, whether the timer is enabled).
  • Reports you send about a question (the reason you write), so we can review the doctrinal accuracy of the content.

d) Catholic Trivia Pro subscription

Payments are processed exclusively by Google Play or the App Store. We never see or store your card details. We receive your subscription status (active, expired, plan) through RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service, linked to your account identifier.

e) Technical data

Like any online service, our infrastructure providers log basic technical data (IP address, device type, operating system and app version) to deliver content, apply updates and keep the service secure. If you enable notifications, a push token is generated for your device so we can send you reminders (for example, the daily challenge). You can turn them off at any time in your phone settings.

f) Website and waitlist

If you leave your email on the triviacatolica.com waitlist, we use it only to tell you when the app is available. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the game: save your progress and streaks and sync them across devices.
  • To show the weekly community leaderboard (only your display name and points).
  • To manage the Pro subscription and its benefits.
  • To review and fix reported questions.
  • To send you notifications, only if you enabled them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Our legal bases are performing the service you request when you use the app, your consent (sign-in, notifications, waitlist) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell or rent your data. We only share it with providers we need to run the app, who process it on our behalf:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, authentication and storage of your account and progress.
Google / AppleSign-in (if you choose it) and payment processing in their stores.
RevenueCatValidation and management of the Pro subscription.
Expo (EAS)App update delivery and push notifications.
VercelHosting of the triviacatolica.com website.

These providers may store data on servers located outside your country. We work with companies that apply recognized security measures. We may also disclose data if required by law or by a competent authority.

News items in the app link to external outlets (such as Vatican News). When you open those links, each site's own privacy policy applies.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account and progress data are kept while your account is active.
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days at most.
  • Question reports may be kept without any link to your account, to maintain content quality.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat according to their legal and tax obligations.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to certain processing and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to hola@triviacatolica.com and we'll reply within 30 days. To delete your account, follow the steps in Delete your account and data. You can also file a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

7. Security

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS), and access to data is restricted by database-level security rules. No system is infallible, but we work hard to protect your information.

8. Children

Catholic Trivia is suitable for all ages and can be played as a family. We don't ask for personal data to play as a guest. If you are a minor, please ask a parent or guardian to help you before signing in or subscribing. If you know a minor has given us data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the “Last updated” date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
