Help and support

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Need help with Catholic Trivia (“Trivia Católica”)? Check the frequently asked questions or email us at hola@triviacatolica.com. We reply within 2 to 3 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign up to play?

No. You can play as a guest right away. If you want to save your progress and keep it when you change phones, sign in with Google or Apple.

I changed phones. How do I get my progress back?

Install the app on your new phone and sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used before. If you played as a guest without signing in, your progress stays only on the old phone.

How do lives work?

On the free plan you have 3 lives that refill automatically every 30 minutes. With Catholic Trivia Pro you get unlimited lives.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Catholic Trivia → Cancel. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Catholic Trivia → Cancel. You keep the benefits until the end of the paid period.

I bought Pro but I don't see the benefits

Make sure you're signed in with the same account you subscribed with, and that your phone uses the same Google Play or Apple account you paid with. Close and reopen the app. If it still doesn't work, email us with your purchase receipt (no card details).

I found a mistake in a question

Thank you! Use the report button on the answer screen. We review every report to keep the content doctrinally accurate.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps in Delete your account and data.
